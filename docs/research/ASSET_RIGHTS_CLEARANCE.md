## Clearance evidence required before import

For each chosen asset class, record the granting legal entity and authorized signatory; exact filenames, versions and SHA-256; original source URL and acquisition date; Android/iOS integrated offline installation; public store, geography, free/paid and in-app purchase scope; whether app bundles can be extracted; allowed conversion, subsetting, scaling and correction processes; notices/attribution; update or withdrawal terms; and written proof that the grant reaches both Complex-origin art and any preparer/maintainer contributions [R1–R4, R7]. Do not treat a reply from one contributor as authority for another contributor's rights. Ask a qualified rights reviewer to interpret any ambiguous grant before owner approval.

**Current clearance conclusion:** QCF4 fonts and QBSML are explicitly unlicensed for Al-Falah redistribution on available evidence [R1]. The Complex's published vector-product terms are more favorable but attach to a separate, edition-unconfirmed artifact [R4]. No production asset may be imported yet [R7].
